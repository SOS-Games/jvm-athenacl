Zenph Studios Yamaha Disklavier Pro Piano Multisamples for OLPC

  A collection of Grand Piano samples played by a Yamaha Disklavier Pro.
  Performed by computer and specifically recorded for OLPC by Dr. Mikhail
  Krishtal, Director of Music Research and Production, and his team at Zenph
  Studios. They are included in the OLPC sound sample library.

  Produced by Zenph Studios in Chapel Hill, North Carolina.
  The main studio location is in Raleigh, North Carolina. http://zenph.com/

Samples from the OLPC sound sample library were donated to Dr. Richard
Boulanger at cSounds.com to support OLPC developers, students, XO users, and
computer and electronic musicians. They are offered under CC BY 3.0.

  http://wiki.laptop.org/go/Sound_samples
  http://csounds.com/boulanger

The installed file is YDP-GrandPiano-20160804.sf2, extracted from
YDP-GrandPiano-SF2-20160804.tar.bz2. The soundfont is not stored in this
repository. `soundbank install freepats-ydp-grand` downloads it and checks
the pinned SHA-256. gm-plus names this instrument piano.grand. The source is
optional: if the file is missing, that name falls through to MuseScore piano
and this credit is not listed, because the bank was not used.
