Upright Piano KW
Version 2022-02-21
FreePats project — https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW

Upright Piano KW by Gonzalo and Roberto for FreePats.

This sound bank has been created from a Kawai upright piano, located in a
living room. Thanks to Inma Martínez de Miguel for kindly letting us access to
her home to play and record the piano.

It was recorded by Gonzalo <humanogonzalo@gmail.com> and Roberto
<roberto@zenvoid.org> on January 2017, using a Zoom H1 portable recorder
mounted on a tripod in front of the piano, approximately at the place where
the head of a piano player would be.

The raw recordings were cropped, edited, and processed by Roberto, using free
software programs.

Published under the terms of Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The installed file is UprightPianoKW-20220221.sf2, extracted from
UprightPianoKW-SF2-20220221.7z. This is the full SF2, not the small edition.
The soundfont is not stored in this repository. `soundbank install freepats-upright-kw`
downloads it and checks the pinned SHA-256.
