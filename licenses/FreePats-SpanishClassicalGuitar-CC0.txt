Spanish Classical Guitar by Roberto for FreePats.

Sound samples recorded from a Spanish classical guitar on 2008. Created by
roberto@zenvoid.org for the FreePats project.

Recorded with an AKG Perception 120 microphone and processed using free
software tools. The recording conditions were far from ideal, so several
filters have been applied to reduce noise and improve sound.

Published under the terms of Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The installed file is SpanishClassicalGuitar-20190618.sf2, extracted from
SpanishClassicalGuitar-SF2-20190618.7z. The soundfont is not stored in this
repository. `soundbank install freepats-spanish-guitar` downloads it and
checks the pinned SHA-256.
